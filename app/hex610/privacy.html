Privacy Policy for hex610

Last updated: 2026-08-19

hex610 is a single-player puzzle game developed by Manfred Scheucher.

hex610 only processes game data (scores, records and progress), exclusively on your device — no data leaves the device; nothing is transmitted or shared. The app uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and requests no network permission — it works fully offline.
