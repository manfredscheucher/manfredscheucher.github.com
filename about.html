About Me

Computer scientist and mathematician with a strong background in solving hard research problems, such as the Empty Hexagon problem by Erdős.

I am interested in algorithms, geometry and computer mathematics, and have used formal methods and solvers extensively, in particular SAT and mixed integer linear programming (MILP) solvers. More recently, I have also been working on cryptography and AI/agents.

I have worked at several research institutions such as TU Graz and TU Berlin; see my publication list.
